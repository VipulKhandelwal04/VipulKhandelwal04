# Hi, I'm Vipul 👋

**Aspiring Product Manager — building at the intersection of AI, distribution, and code.**

I started in civil engineering, fell for the way data explains *why* things happen, and I'm now channeling that into product. My goal: become a PM who doesn't just write specs, but prototypes ideas, measures them, and obsesses over how they reach the people who need them.
---
   
🎯 The kind of PM I'm becoming

I think about product through three lenses, and I'm deliberately building reps in each:

- 🤖 **AI PM** — I want to build products where the model *is* the experience. I'm getting fluent in what today's LLMs can and can't do, prototyping with them directly, and learning the unglamorous parts:
evals, latency, cost, and earning user trust.
- 📡 **Distribution PM** — A great product nobody can find isn't a great product. I'm learning to think in growth loops, activation, and channels — how something gets discovered, adopted, and kept.
- 🛠️  **Builder PM** — I'd rather ship a rough prototype than win a slide. I'm building enough hands-on skill to validate an idea myself instead of waiting in an engineering queue.
